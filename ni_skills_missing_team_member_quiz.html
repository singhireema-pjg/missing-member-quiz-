Scenario: The Missing Team Member

You are part of the student council at Greenfield School. The school is selecting five students for an inter-school science challenge, and an AI tool has been asked to recommend the team using students' previous examination marks.

The AI selects five students with the highest scores and leaves out Aarav, whose marks dropped last term. However, Aarav had been recovering from an illness and missed two weeks of school. Since returning, he has improved his marks, built an impressive working science model and shown strong problem-solving skills.

The AI was not given information about his illness, recent progress or practical abilities.

What the AI recommended: “Do not select Aarav. His previous marks are lower than those of the other shortlisted students.”

Your challenge: Should the school follow the AI recommendation, question it, or use a better method to select the team?

Ni Skills takeaway: AI can help organise information, but previous marks may not show the full picture. People should check relevant evidence, consider context and remain responsible for the final decision.

Remember the human-in-charge approach

  1. Question: What information did the AI use?
  2. Check: What relevant context or evidence may be missing?
  3. Decide responsibly: Consider fairness, team needs and the evidence before making a final decision.
